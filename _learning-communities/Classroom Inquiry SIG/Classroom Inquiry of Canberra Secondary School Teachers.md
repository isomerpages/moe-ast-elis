<p>In 2024, two EL professional learning teams (PLTs) inquired into assessment
feedback, supported by Dr Rachel Goh (SS/EL).</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Spotlight 1: Empowering Students with GenAI Feedback</strong>
</p>
<p>The team led by Ms Wong Sooh Yee (HOD/EL) and Mr Ghazali B Abdul Wahab
(LT/EL) explored how GenAI feedback could help students become more self-regulated
during the writing process.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Inquiry focus</strong>
</p>
<p>The team developed two chatbots — <a href="https://poe.com/CaptainHook_CBSS" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Captain Hook</a> and <a href="https://poe.com/CaptainPEEL_CBSS" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Captain PEEL</a> —
to guide students in obtaining feedback on their introductory paragraph
and body paragraphs respectively.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Key insights</strong>
</p>
<p>The teachers realised the importance of equipping learners to use GenAI
tools effectively. This preparation would include guiding students to craft
effective prompts that generate targeted feedback, helping them to interpret
and understand GenAI feedback, and teaching them to prioritise which feedback
suggestions to implement when revising their writing.</p>
<p>Informed by their inquiry, Mr Ghazali, Ms Victoria Teo, Mr Mohamed Nor
Ariffin Ismail and Ms Sumaya Jahan designed and conducted a Teacher-led
Workshop (TLW) on Empowering Students: AI-generated Feedback as A Catalyst
for Developing Students’ Self-regulation Skills at the Academy of Singapore
Teachers (AST) on 25 February 2025.</p>

<p><strong>Spotlight 2: Success Criteria and Peer Feedback in Situational Writing</strong>
</p>
<p>Led by Ms Vishnu Preyei, the team comprising Mr Benjamin Chong Wai Jian,
Ms Joann David and Ms Nur Fatin Nordin, with advice from Ms Diyanah Binte
Mohamad Yunos (LH/EL), focused on co-constructing success criteria and
peer feedback to help students develop content for situational writing.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>The inquiry focus</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Pre-Writing:</strong>&nbsp;Teachers and students analysed exemplars
together to co-construct clear&nbsp;success criteria.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Post-Writing:</strong>&nbsp;Students offered and received peer
feedback guided by a structured checklist.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Key insights</strong>
</p>
<p>The teachers realised that lower-readiness students benefited from 'clarification
loops' – opportunities to seek clarification from peers and/or teachers
before revising their work. The inquiry highlighted the value of giving
students space to articulate their thinking processes and reflect on specific
feedback, enabling them to make more purposeful revisions to their writing.</p>
