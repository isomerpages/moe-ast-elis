<p><strong>How can the integration of AI-generated feedback practices in English Language writing instruction help students plan, monitor and revise their writing?</strong>
</p>
<p>In 2025,&nbsp;Mdm Lynn Loy (LT/EL)&nbsp;and her professional learning
team (PLT), comprising Ms Jumiah Bte Ahmad, Ms Mavis Ho, Ms Nazrene Akbar
Ali, Ms Maryam Anuar, and Ms Norhafizah Maarof, set out to answer this
question. Supported by&nbsp;Dr Rachel Goh (SS/EL), they embarked on a classroom
inquiry to investigate the impact of AI-generated feedback on students’
learning in the writing process.</p>
<p><strong>Inquiry focus</strong>
</p>
<p>The team explored how integrating GenAI tools — specifically&nbsp;Scribo&nbsp;and
Claro chatbot — into writing lessons could enhance students’ planning,
monitoring and revision processes. The goal was to leverage GenAI to support
a metacognitive approach to writing development.</p>
<p><strong>Key insights</strong>
</p>
<p>With teacher guidance, students moved away from passively accepting GenAI
suggestions and began critically evaluating the feedback to make informed
decisions for their writing. Over time, they learnt to engage GenAI more
strategically. They also learnt to critique GenAI feedback thus resulting
in stronger student voice and agency.</p>
<p><strong>Contributing to the fraternity</strong>
</p>
<p>To communicate their findings, the team developed an&nbsp;<a href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jhmS1ltPvfZAD5w9uJDts52fXTQlxn5H/view" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">infographic</a>&nbsp;detailing
the roles of both teachers and students when engaging with GenAI in the
writing process. This resource is featured in the&nbsp;<a href="https://go.gov.sg/ai-ell" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">EL Secondary Quick Guide to GenAI</a>,
serving as a practical reference for educators looking to integrate GenAI
meaningfully in their own classrooms.</p>
