<p>On Thursday, 2 April 2026, members of the&nbsp;ELIS Classroom Inquiry
Special Interest Group (SIG)&nbsp;met in person to celebrate the successful
completion of their classroom inquiry projects. The event showcased the
dedication of three teams whose studies yielded encouraging outcomes for
both teachers and students involved.</p>

<p><strong>Highlights of the session</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Knowledge Building:</strong>&nbsp;Participants gained valuable
insights from the inquiry findings, exploring practical applications for
the EL classroom.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Productive Discussion:&nbsp;</strong>A lively Q&amp;A segment
followed each presentation, allowing teachers to clarify learning points
and exchange ideas for effective teaching.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Collaborative Networking:&nbsp;</strong>The session concluded
with breakout groups where members networked and brainstormed focus areas
for their next cycle of inquiry.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Looking ahead</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>For members eager to embark on their next classroom inquiry,&nbsp;<strong>ELIS Specialists&nbsp;</strong>provide
support to guide the inquiry process.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The SIG reconvenes later this year for a&nbsp;<strong>Progress Update&nbsp;</strong>session.
This upcoming milestone offers members a platform to gather constructive
feedback from both SIG peers and specialists, ensuring that their inquiry
process remains robust and impactful.</p>
</li>
</ul>
